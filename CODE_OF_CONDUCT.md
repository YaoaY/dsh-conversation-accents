# Code Of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully;
- focus criticism on code and behavior, not people;
- provide reproducible technical information;
- respect privacy and remove private conversation data from reports;
- accept maintainers' decisions about scope and release safety.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when these standards are violated.
